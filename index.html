Sanzo Pairings

Three-color combinations from Wada Sanzo's Dictionary of Color Combinations, checked against your Deep Autumn palette.

Pick a color

"In your palette" is an estimate from each color's lightness, warmth and saturation, based on the Deep Autumn read of your photos. Use it as a guide.

Color values and combination numbers follow Wada Sanzo's Dictionary of Color Combinations, via the open dataset by Matt DesLauriers (mattdesl/dictionary-of-colour-combinations). Screen colors differ from fabric, so check swatches in daylight.
